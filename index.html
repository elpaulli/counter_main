The Experience

counter- is Michelin starred tasting menu concept by Chef and innovator Sam Hart. We are a story-driven dining experience focused on producing the highest level personal memory for guests, while showcasing our region in a singular composition. Owned and operated as a collective effort amongst employees with a shared mission to change the industry, counter- is designed to evoke emotion throughout the progressive dining experience, fully immersing guests by utilizing sound, taste, and art as a vehicle. The menu, which rotates quarterly, is driven by sense-based storylines such as music, modern art, and mental health.

The Design

Purposefully designed to mimic a blank canvas and features muted concrete floors and countertops that can easily be transformed to fit the rotating menus, tableware, and art that accompany each quarterly story-driven menu.

The Details

Similar to a theatrical performance, counter- is a shared experience with seatings Wednesday through Saturday. Seating is limited and reservations are required.

Wine and spirit-free beverage pairings are available.

Please be respectful of all parties during your seating. For special events, business dinners, and other large parties, our private dining room may be the perfect choice.

Certain seasonal themes may not be suitable for children under the age of 18, so please be sure to contact us in advance at reservations@counterclt.com.

The Menus

At counter- we approach our menus as a crime-writer might approach a plot or the way a composer might approach the second movement.

Because of the creative energy that goes into every tasting menu at counter-, we like to call them “themes.” Our themes change every season, and we hold specialty menus sporadically throughout the year! No two themes are the same, and courses are never repeated after the end of the three-month period, ensuring a novel experience with each visit.
